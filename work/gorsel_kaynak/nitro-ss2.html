Description & Specifications

NITRO® #08B-2 double strand metric roller chain is a high-performance solution for applications demanding durability and corrosion resistance. Engineered from premium stainless steel, this chain is built to withstand harsh environments. Its double strand design provides enhanced strength and load capacity, making it ideal for many industrial applications.

08BSS-2 Roller Chain Dimensions

  • Pitch (P):12.70mm
  • Inner Width (b1):7.75mm
  • Roller Diameter (d1):8.51mm
  • Overall Width (L):36.10mm
  • Pin Diameter (d2):4.45mm
  • Link Plate Height (h2):11.80mm
  • Link Plate Thickness (T):1.60mm
  • No. Of Pitches/Links Per 10 Feet:240
  • No. Of Connecting Links Included:1
  • Chain Material:304 Stainless Steel

All dimensions listed are nominal values.
